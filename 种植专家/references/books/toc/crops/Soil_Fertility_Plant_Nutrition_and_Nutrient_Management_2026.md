- About the Editor
- Lin Tang
-     Lin Tang is a researcher participating in the Sustainable Field Crops Program at the Institute of
- Agrifood Research and Technology (IRTA), Spain. Her research focuses on evaluating ecosystem
- services to promote legume cultivation across Europe.      Her primary interest lies in leveraging
- plant–soil–microbe interactions to develop cropping systems with high protein quality and low
- nutrient emissions, while investigating the underlying mechanisms inﬂuencing improved crop
- performance, nutrient cycling and overall system resilience. She also has extensive experience in
- soil organic carbon sequestration, greenhouse gas mitigation, forage production, phytoremediation
- and biofortiﬁcation.
