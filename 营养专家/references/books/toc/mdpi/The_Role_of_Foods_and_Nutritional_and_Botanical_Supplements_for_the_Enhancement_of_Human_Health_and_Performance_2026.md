- Robert M. Hackman
-     Robert M. Hackman is a nutritionist in the Department of Nutrition at the University of
- California, Davis. His research addresses the role of foods and nutritional and botanical supplements
- for enhancement of human health and performance. His work on dietary supplements includes
- a focus on medicinal mushroom mycelia, and the authentication of botanical extracts.            Prof.
- Hackman’s research on foods includes the role of fruits, nuts, wine and berries on vascular function,
- inﬂammation, skin aging and eye health.
