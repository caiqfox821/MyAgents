- About the Editors
- Aleksandra Bojarczuk
-      Aleksandra Bojarczuk is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Molecular Biology at Gdansk
- University of Physical Education and Sport, Poland. Her research interests include sports genetics,
- molecular biology, immunology, exercise physiology, and the molecular determinants of responses to
- physical activity, sports injury susceptibility, and training adaptation. She has participated in nationally
- funded research projects concerning natural polyphenol-rich extracts and environmental exposures
- among military personnel. Currently, she contributes to a project investigating athletes’ genetic proﬁles
- and gut microbiome. She is a co-author of a scientiﬁc monograph. She serves as Second Associate
- Editor of the Baltic Journal of Health and Physical Activity, a member of the Topical Advisory Panel
- for Molecular Genetics and Genomics, an Ambassador for the International Journal of Molecular Sciences,
- and an Early Career Editorial Board Member and Guest Editor for Applied Sciences. Her distinctions
- include the Amber Laurel Award from the Rector of Gdansk University of Physical Education and
- Sport and awards for oral and poster presentations at international scientiﬁc conferences.
- Monika Michałowska-Sawczyn
-      Monika Michałowska-Sawczyn is an Associate Professor at the Department of Physical Education
- at Gdansk University of Physical Education and Sport, Poland. Her research interests include sports
- genetics, psychogenetics, molecular determinants of exercise adaptation, genetic predisposition to
- sports performance and injury susceptibility, and the relationship between physical activity, personality
- traits, and gut microbiota. She has participated in research projects investigating genetic variants
- associated with athletes’ psychological characteristics, physical performance and responses to exercise.
- She is a co-author of numerous scientiﬁc publications on dopamine-related gene polymorphisms,
- personality traits and athletic performance. She serves as the 4th Associate Editor of the Baltic Journal of
- Health and Physical Activity and has acted as a Guest Editor for Applied Sciences. Her scientiﬁc activities
- focus on the role of genetic and molecular factors in sports performance, training adaptation, and
- athletes’ health.
