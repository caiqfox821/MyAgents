- About the Editors
- Ching-Hsein Chen
-      Ching-Hsein Chen is a professor in the Department of Microbiology, Immunology and
- Biopharmaceuticals at National Chiayi University, Taiwan. His research interests focus on cancer
- biology, molecular signaling pathways, oxidative stress, apoptosis, autophagy, and the anticancer
- activities of natural compounds and combination therapies. His recent studies have particularly
- emphasized the roles of MAPK signaling, PI3K/Akt pathways, NF-κB, and reactive oxygen species
- in colorectal cancer and osteosarcoma models. He has published numerous peer-reviewed articles
- related to dietary phytochemicals and anticancer mechanisms and currently serves as a Guest Editor
- and reviewer for several international scientiﬁc journals.
- Yi-Wen Liu
-      Yi-Wen Liu is afﬁliated with the Department of Microbiology,              Immunology and
- Biopharmaceuticals, and serves as the CEO of the Integrated Medicine and Pharmacy Science
- Master Program at National Chiayi University, Taiwan. Her research focuses on anticancer drug
- development, as well as studies of herbal medicine and acupuncture in tumor-bearing mouse
- models. She is also interested in interactions between tumor-associated macrophage and cancer
- cells.   Professor Liu has published numerous peer-reviewed articles related to bladder cancer
- and anticancer compounds, and she currently serves as a Guest Editor and reviewer for several
- international scientiﬁc journals.
