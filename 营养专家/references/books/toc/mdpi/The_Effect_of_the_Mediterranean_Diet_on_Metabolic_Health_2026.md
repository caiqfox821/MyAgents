- About the Editors
- Alessandro Leone
-      Alessandro Leone is Associate Professor at the Department of Food, Environmental and
- Nutritional Sciences, University of Milan, Italy. His research focuses on human nutrition, dietetics,
- nutritional assessment, metabolic health, and the relationship between dietary patterns, food quality,
- and health outcomes. He is afﬁliated with the International Center for the Assessment of Nutritional
- Status for the Development of Interventional Studies (ICANS-DIS) and has contributed to national
- and international research on topics including Mediterranean diet, obesity and cardiometabolic risk,
- ultra-processed foods, glycemic control, renal nutrition, and plant-based bioactive compounds. He
- teaches in the ﬁeld of nutrition and health promotion and has authored numerous peer-reviewed
- scientiﬁc publications in nutrition, metabolism, and public health.
- Ramona Silvana De Amicis
-      Ramona Silvana De Amicis is dietitian and Assistant Professor at the Department of Food,
- Environmental and Nutritional Sciences, University of Milan, Italy. She holds a PhD in Nutrition
- Sciences and her scientiﬁc activity focuses on clinical nutrition, dietary strategies, nutritional status
- assessment, and the role of diet in the prevention and management of chronic and metabolic diseases.
- Her research interests include dietary patterns, obesity, body composition, cardiometabolic risk,
- and personalized nutritional interventions. She is involved in teaching activities in nutrition and
- health promotion and contributes to research and public engagement initiatives aimed at translating
- nutritional science into evidence-based approaches for health and disease prevention.

- Preface
-      The Mediterranean diet continues to represent one of the most widely studied and clinically
- relevant dietary patterns for the promotion of health and the prevention of chronic diseases. This
- Reprint was prepared with the aim of bringing together recent evidence on its relationship with
- metabolic health, highlighting both consolidated knowledge and emerging research perspectives.
