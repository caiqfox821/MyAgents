- Preface
-      This Reprint is dedicated to the evolving role of nutrition in advanced chronic liver disease and
- portal hypertension. Its scope includes the assessment and management of malnutrition, sarcopenia,
- obesity, metabolic dysfunction, micronutrient deﬁciencies, and nutrition-related complications in
- patients with cirrhosis, portal hypertension, and liver transplantation.
-      The aim is to provide clinicians and researchers with an updated collection of scientiﬁc
- contributions addressing how nutritional factors inﬂuence disease progression, clinical outcomes,
- and therapeutic strategies in hepatology. The motivation for assembling these papers arises from the
- increasing recognition that nutrition is not merely supportive care, but a central component of risk
- stratiﬁcation, personalized treatment, and multidisciplinary management.
-      This Reprint is addressed to hepatologists, gastroenterologists, clinical nutrition specialists,
- transplant physicians, dietitians, and investigators working in liver disease, metabolism, and
- patient-centered care.
