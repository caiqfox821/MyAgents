- About the Editor
- Samuel Fernández-Tomé
-     Samuel Fernández-Tomé has a degree in Veterinary with honors from Complutense University
- of Madrid (UCM, 2011) and MSc at the Department of Nutrition, Bromatology and Food Technology
- (UCM, 2012). PhD in Biology and Food Science focused on the gastrointestinal digestion of food
- proteins, dietary bioactive peptides, and their effects on digestive health, at the Institute of Food
- Science Research (CIAL, CSIC-UAM, 2012–2016) and granted a competitive PhD fellowship from
- the Spanish Ministry of Economy and Competitiveness. PhD internships (ICTAN-CSIC, URJC,
- IdiPAZ, and EEZ-CSIC from Spain; and University of Massachusetts from the USA, granted by the
- International Mobility FPI-Program). Postdoctoral stage at the Health Research Institute of Hospital
- University La Princesa in the Inﬂammatory Bowel Disease Unit, granted by the competitive Sara
- Borrell postdoctoral fellowship (Instituto de Salud Carlos III, 2017–2021). Participation in more than
- 20 competitive research projects (1 as PI), and contribution to more than 50 national or international
- conferences. Accredited as Assistant Professor and Associate Professor by The National Agency for
- Quality Assessment and Accreditation of Spain (ANECA). In November 2021, incorporated into the
- Department of Nutrition and Food Science (Faculty of Pharmacy, UCM). Teaching responsibilities at
- UCM in the Pharmacy, Food Science and Technology, and Human Nutrition and Dietetics degrees,
- as well as in the Dual degree, Pharmacy-Human Nutrition and Dietetics. Multidisciplinary research
- activity mainly focused on the role of food-derived bioactive compounds at the gastrointestinal level.
