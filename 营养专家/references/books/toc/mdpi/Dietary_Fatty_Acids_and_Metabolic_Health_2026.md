- About the Editor
- Éva Szabó
-     Éva Szabó is a senior lecturer at the Department of Biochemistry and Medical Chemistry, Medical
- School, University of Pécs, Pécs, Hungary. She teaches medical chemistry and biochemistry, actively
- contributing to the education of future physicians and dietitians. Her research focuses on fatty
- acid metabolism and its role in human health. Her earlier studies examined lipid proﬁles during
- the perinatal period, including human milk, and maternal and infant blood, to better understand
- nutritional and developmental processes. More recently, her work has expanded to investigate
- dietary fatty acid intake and the fatty acid composition of various food items, aiming to connect
- biochemical insights with practical nutrition. Dr. Szabó is a member of the Hungarian Young
- Academy and has been honored with the Excellent Author Award by the University of Pécs in 2023
- and 2024 for her scientiﬁc contributions.
