- Preface
-      Obesity is a global epidemic and a chronic, multifactorial disease with profound impacts on
- metabolic, cardiovascular, and overall health. This reprint of Biomolecules explores the latest scientiﬁc
- advancements in understanding and addressing obesity. Recent research has uncovered complex
- interactions between genetic, molecular, environmental, and behavioral factors, revealing new
- biomarkers and therapeutic targets1-4 . The reprint covers innovative pharmacological approaches5,6 ,
- the role of the immune system7,8,9 , gut–brain axis communication7,10 , molecular understanding11, the
- role of ultra-processed food12 , and the role of personalized nutrition13 and digital health technologies
- in obesity management1-4 .
-      The aim of the current work is to bridge the gap between groundbreaking research and clinical
- practice. By presenting cutting-edge insights into molecular mechanisms, this Reprint seeks to
- empower clinicians, researchers, and policymakers to develop evidence-based, multidisciplinary
- strategies.   The purpose is to foster collaboration across ﬁelds—from endocrinology to public
- health—to create sustainable, equitable solutions for obesity and its comorbidities.
