- Preface
-     Obesity and weight loss treatments remain central challenges for nutrition science, clinical
- practice and public health. This Reprint was prepared to gather contributions that examine obesity
- beyond a single-discipline perspective, integrating dietary approaches, anthropometry, biomarkers,
- body composition, molecular nutrition, cultural factors and new technologies. Its purpose is to
- provide readers with a concise and updated collection of studies and reviews that may support
- safer, more personalized and evidence-based strategies for weight management. The Reprint is
- addressed to researchers, clinicians, dietitians, public health professionals and students interested
- in the prevention and treatment of obesity.
