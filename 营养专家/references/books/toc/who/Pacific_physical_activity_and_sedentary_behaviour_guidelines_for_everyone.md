-     Background
-     Physical activity is critical to human health. Regular physical activity helps to prevent and manage a variety of health
-     conditions, improves mood and sleep, boosts energy, and supports healthy ageing. Adopting a healthy lifestyle
-     that includes regular physical activity is one of the most important things that people can do to improve health and
-     well-being.
