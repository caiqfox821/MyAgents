- Preface to ”Brewing and Craft Beer”
-      Beer is a beverage with more than 8000 years of history, and the process of brewing has not
- changed much over the centuries. However, important technical advances have allowed us to
- produce beer in a more sophisticated and efﬁcient way. The proliferation of specialty hop varieties has
- been behind the popularity of craft beers seen in the past few years around the world. Craft brewers
- interpret historic beer with unique styles. Craft beers are undergoing an unprecedented period of
- growth, and more than 150 beer styles are currently recognized. While many studies have suggested
