- About the Editor
- Carina Ladeira
-     Carina Ladeira is PhD in Biology with specialization in Genetics and Master in Human
- Molecular Biology from the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon and has a 4-year degree in
- Biomedical Laboratorial Sciences. She is Full Professor at the Lisbon School of Health Technology
- in the Biomedical Laboratory Sciences degree and in the Master’s Degree in Clinical-Laboratory
- Technologies, collaborating also in other postgraduate programs. She has participated in several
- international and national scientiﬁc projects, author of 1 book, 8 book chapters, around 40 scientiﬁc
- articles in journals with an impact factor, 50 oral communications and 100 poster communications.
- She was member of scientiﬁc and organizing committees for several national and international
- events, and served as moderator of roundtables at scientiﬁc events. She is European Registered
- Toxicologist (ERT) and has been expert evaluator of scientiﬁc projects at international level. She
- is the Chair of the International Comet Assay Working Group (ICAWG), a satellite group of the
- European Environmental Mutagenesis and Genomics Society (EEMGS). Her research interests are
- focused in human biomonitoring, genetic toxicology, biomarkers, chemicals mixtures, exposome, risk
- assessment, carcinogenesis and prevention.
