# Response to Reviewers 模板

> 用法：逐条回复，不遗漏；标注修改位置（页码/行号/章节）。未采纳须说明理由，保持尊重。

---

# Response to Reviewers

**Manuscript:** [Title]
**Manuscript ID:** [MS ID]
**Journal:** [Journal Name]

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

We thank the editor and the reviewers for their constructive comments. We have revised the manuscript accordingly. Our point-by-point responses are below; reviewer comments are in *italics*, our responses follow. Revised text locations are given as page/line numbers in the revised manuscript.

## 生成约束
- 逐条对应，编号与审稿意见一致。
- 回复与稿件实际修改一致（不得声称已改而未改）。
- 不争辩、不敷衍；不编造实验或数据。
- 引用参考文献时核对真实存在与正确格式。
