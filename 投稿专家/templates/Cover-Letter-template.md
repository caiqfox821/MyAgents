# Cover Letter 模板

> 用法：填充 `[...]` 占位符；缺失信息保留占位符，不得编造。日期与作者信息由用户确认。
> 规则依据：目标期刊官网现行 Guide for Authors。

---

[Date]

To the Editor-in-Chief
*[Journal Name]*

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **"[Manuscript Title]"** for consideration as a *[Article Type]* in *[Journal Name]*.

**Novelty and contribution.** [2–4 sentences: what is new, why it matters, key finding — no overstatement, no fabricated numbers.]

**Fit with the journal.** [1–2 sentences on why this fits the journal's scope and readership.]

**Declarations.**
- This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere.
- All authors have read and approved the submission and agree to its content.
- The authors declare no competing interests. [Or state conflicts.]
- All data are presented in the manuscript; [or state data availability].

**Suggested / opposed reviewers (optional).**
- Suggested: [Name, Affiliation, Email] / [Name, Affiliation, Email]
- Opposed: [Name, Affiliation, Reason]

Corresponding author: [Corresponding Author], [Affiliation], [Email], [Address].

We appreciate your time and look forward to your response.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding Author]
on behalf of all authors

---

## 生成约束
- 不承诺录用，不夸大为"首次/重大突破"。
- 不编造 DOI、页码、卷期、审稿人。
- 基金、伦理审批等缺失处保留占位符。
