- About the Editor
- Jingran Bi
- Jingran Bi is an associate professor at School of Food Science and Technology, Dalian Polytechnic
- University, China. She focuses on the research of food quality control and has published over 90
- papers, which have been cited over 1000 times with an H-index of 19. She possesses considerable
- experience in taking charge of national projects and currently oversees a Young Scientists Fund of
- National Natural Science Foundation of China. Additionally, she serves as a guest editor of Foods
- (MDPI) and holds a director position at Liaoning Food Science and Technology Society.
