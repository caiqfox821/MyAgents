- Preface
- Functional foods have become an important area of research because they offer opportunities
- to combine good nutrition with added health-related beneﬁts. At the same time, developing such
- products requires a careful balance between nutritional value, technological quality, sensory properties,
- and consumer expectations.
- This Reprint brings together studies that explore different aspects of functional food development,
- from the use of innovative and bioactive ingredients to the evaluation of nutritional, technological,
- and antioxidant properties. The contributions reﬂect the diversity of current research in this ﬁeld and
- show how different scientiﬁc approaches can be used to improve the quality and functionality of food
- products.
- Our aim was to provide a useful collection of recent research for scientists, food technologists,
- nutrition professionals, and others interested in the development and evaluation of functional foods.
- We hope that this Reprint will encourage further research, new ideas, and practical applications that
- can contribute to the development of high-quality, nutritious, and innovative food products.
- Jovana Petrović, Ivana Nikolić, and Dragana Šoronja-Simović
