- About the Editor
- Ahmed M. Khalil
- Ahmed M. Khalil is a senior researcher at the Photochemistry Department - National Research
- Center (NRC), Egypt. He is dedicated to the ﬁelds of polymer science and photochemistry. His work
- focuses on enhancing the performance of polymers through advanced tailoring techniques, including
- the development of membranes for water remediation and composites modiﬁed with nanomaterials
- or antimicrobial substrates. These innovations aim to improve material functionality for a wide range
- of applications, reﬂecting his commitment to both scientiﬁc rigor and applied relevance. In addition
- to his research, he has made signiﬁcant contributions to numerous peer-reviewed publications,
- patents, and collaborative projects focusing on polymer recycling and waste management for a
- greener environment. His efforts are driven by a vision to advance materials science to support
- ecological sustainability and industrial innovation, bridging fundamental research with practical
- solutions.
